Workout Program Comparison

The table below provides an example of how different training styles can fit into a weekly fitness routine.

Comparison of Fitness Training Programs
Program Primary Goal Days Per Week Example Exercises
Strength Build strength and muscle 3-4 days Squats, presses, deadlifts
Conditioning Improve endurance 2-4 days Running, rowing, cycling
Hybrid Build strength and endurance 3-5 days Weightlifting and cardio

How to Choose a Program

Consider the following when selecting your training routine:

  • Your primary fitness goal
  • Your current training experience
  • How many days you can train each week
  • Available equipment and facilities
  • Time available for recovery
