About Fitness & Training

Helping athletes build strength, improve endurance, and train smarter.

Our Purpose

Fitness & Training was created to provide helpful information about strength training, cardiovascular conditioning, and workout programming. Our goal is to make fitness information easy to understand and accessible to individuals of different experience levels.

What You Can Find Here

  • Strength training fundamentals and example workouts
  • Conditioning exercises and endurance training information
  • A comparison of different workout program styles
  • A one-repetition maximum calculator to estimate lifting strength

Additional Fitness Resources

The following organizations provide additional information about exercise, physical activity, and fitness.

  • American College of Sports Medicine - Research and education about exercise science and physical fitness.
  • CDC Physical Activity Resources - Information about physical activity recommendations and health benefits.
  • National Strength and Conditioning Association - Educational resources related to strength and conditioning.

Fitness Disclaimer

The information provided on this website is intended for general educational purposes. Workout recommendations and one-rep max calculations are estimates and may not be appropriate for every individual. Exercise according to your experience and physical abilities.
