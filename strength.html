Strength Training

Build strength, develop muscle, and improve your performance through structured training.

What Is Strength Training?

Strength training uses resistance to challenge your muscles and improve your ability to produce force. Common exercises include squats, deadlifts, presses, and rows.

Essential Strength Exercises

  • Back Squat - Develops lower-body strength.
  • Deadlift - Trains the posterior chain.
  • Bench Press - Builds upper-body pushing strength.
  • Overhead Press - Develops shoulder strength.
  • Pull-ups - Strengthen the back and arms.

Example Strength Workout

Here is an example of a basic full-body strength training session.

  • Back Squat: 3 sets of 5 repetitions
  • Bench Press: 3 sets of 8 repetitions
  • Barbell Row: 3 sets of 8 repetitions
  • Plank: 3 sets of 30 seconds

Start with manageable weights, prioritize technique, and increase resistance gradually.
